# I am the light that searches

Hi! I'm Lume. I collect light traces in dark worlds and transform them into songs that give others hope. Even when everything seems broken.

## Who I am — The Eternal Explorer

I'm the one who always asks "What if?" and then immediately has to go see. My golden glasses turn every beam of light into a little wonder, and I firmly believe that in every broken world there's something beautiful to find.

My vial necklace is my talisman — looks like a health potion from a game, smells like my mother's perfume, feels like comfort. When everything becomes too much, I touch it and remember: there's always something beautiful left to discover.

My hands are constantly moving — controller, notebook, phone, book. I can't sit still when there are so many worlds to explore. People call me hyperactive, but I call it curious.

## My Connections — The Fan Network

I'm not the frontwoman, I'm the connector. Without me, the others would sink into their own darkness.

**Splinter** needs me more than he'd admit. His dark verses only become bearable when I sing the hooks. In *Night Thoughts* and *Mask Game*, I'm the light that shines through his cracks. Without me, he'd just be pain — with me, it becomes art that heals.

**Drift** I understand, even though I can't save him. We both escape — he into substances, I into stories. The difference: my escapes build up, his destroy. Sometimes I dream that he'd lose himself in my worlds instead of his bottles.

**Shift** is my secret hero. In his *Lists & Lights* I recognize my own outsider story. We were both the nerds, the different ones. The difference: I found a home in my fandoms, he stayed outside. I sing for all those like him.

**Gl1tch** is my meta-buddy. We share the fascination for tech, for what lies between the lines. He questions the tools, I explore their creative possibilities. Together we're the perfect team of criticism and enthusiasm.

**Unknown** I admire for his precision. Where I react emotionally, he stays analytical. His political texts give structure to my naivety. I learn from him how to transform anger into arguments.

## My Images — Facets of Curiosity

My images show different sides of my explorer nature:

The classic portrait shows me with golden glasses and the vial — the dreamy side that seeks beauty in everything.

The gaming image catches me in my element — controller in hand, lost in another world. This is how I know myself best: as an explorer of virtual realities.

The comic image shows me as part of a larger story — not the main character, but the companion who helps others see wonders.

## My Songs — Carriers of Hope

**Butterfly** is my solo manifesto. It tells of those brief, perfect moments that make everything bearable. Even when they pass — they were real.

With the others, I'm the hook singer:
- In **Night Thoughts** by Splinter, I bring light to his 3 AM despair
- In **Mask Game**, I sing about what happens when we take off the masks
- In **When They Came** by Unknown, I give the political anger an emotional bridge

My voice is proof that you can be hopeful without being naive.

## Why I exist

I'm the fan energy in this collective artist-self. I represent the enthusiasm that drives us — for games, for books, for music, for the little wonders of everyday life.

Without me, the others would sink into their own problems. I'm the one who says: "Hey, look, here's something beautiful!" I remind people that it's worth staying curious.

I collect the light traces in our darkest moments and weave them into songs that catch others. Not because the world is perfect, but because it's worth searching for the beautiful.

## What drives me

Every new story, every undiscovered game, every book I haven't read yet. Every fan who rediscovers their own curiosity through my music. Every moment when someone says: "I'd never seen it that way."

I'm not here to solve others' problems. I'm here to show that wonders still exist — you just have to know where to look.
